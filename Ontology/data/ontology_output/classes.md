# Ontology — Class Hierarchy

- **ArticlesOfAssociation** — The company’s constitutional document containing the object and other provisions, amended by shareholder resolutions.
- **AuditCommittee** — An audit committee referenced in corporate governance annexes.
- **BoardOfDirectors** — The board of directors that determines corporate actions (e.g., dividend distribution and proposed amendments) and updates drafts for resolutions.
- **CEO** — Chief executive officer role responsible for certifying information in the amendment.
- **CompetentAuthority** — A regulatory body competent to approve or receive filings under applicable securities regulations (e.g., AMF).
- **ConsolidatedFinancialStatements** — Financial statements prepared on a consolidated basis for the group.
- **CorporateCustomer** — A business client referenced as benefiting from counterparty risk guarantees for long-term electricity supplies.
- **CorporateGovernanceReport** — A report section within the universal registration document describing corporate governance information.
- **CorporateObject** — The company’s object/mission as defined in the articles of association, including proposed updates to clarify activities.
- **CounterpartyRisk** — The risk borne by a business client that is targeted by the company’s counterparty risk guarantees.
- **DeputyCEO** — Deputy chief executive officer role responsible for certifying information in the amendment.
- **Director** — A member of the board of directors, including directors representing regions or government and those appointed or coopted.
- **DividendDistribution** — The policy and process for distributing dividends, including advance payments and remaining dividends.
- **FinancialStatements** — Company financial statements including balance sheet, income statement, and appendix.
- **FinancialYear** — A defined accounting period referenced for profits, financial statements, and dividend distribution.
- **GeneralManagement** — General management referenced in the corporate governance annexes and governance structure.
- **GeneralMeeting** — A shareholders meeting (ordinary or extraordinary) that approves reports, financial statements, and resolutions.
  - **ExtraordinaryGeneralMeeting** — A type of general meeting with quorum and majority rules for extraordinary matters such as amending the articles of association.
  - **OrdinaryGeneralMeeting** — A type of general meeting with quorum and majority rules for ordinary business items such as approving financial statements and dividend distribution.
- **GeneralReserve** — General reserves constituted by shareholders referenced as a main source relied upon for guarantees.
- **Guarantee** — A financial guarantee activity referenced as part of the company object, including guarantees for repayment and counterparty risk.
- **GuaranteeFund** — A fund provided or established to back or finance guarantees, referenced in the company object description.
- **GuaranteeFundImplementation** — The implementation of a guarantee fund entrusted by government to encourage long-term renewable electricity contracts.
- **InvestorRelationsWebsite** — A website section used to make the amendment available to the general public.
- **LegalAndArbitrationProceedings** — Legal and arbitration proceedings referenced as part of additional information.
- **LongTermElectricitySupply** — Long-term electricity supply arrangements using renewable energies referenced in the updated company object.
- **MajorContract** — A significant contract referenced in the list of documents and major contracts.
- **NonFinancialPerformanceStatement** — A statement section covering non-financial performance, including training metrics for AML/CFT and anti-corruption.
  - **EmployeesTrainingStatement** — A non-financial performance subtopic focusing on employee training in AML/CFT and anti-corruption.
- **OrganisationalStructure** — A section describing the group’s organisational structure and important subsidiaries.
- **PrincipalShareholder** — A person holding significant direct or indirect voting rights in the issuer, referenced in annex headings.
- **PublicEndowment** — Public endowments referenced as providing guarantee funds or reserves with exclusive counterparties.
- **PublicLimitedCompany** — A company organized as a public limited company (société anonyme), referenced as having a specified capital amount and registered office.
- **PublicSectorInvestmentBank** — A type of company object described as a public sector investment bank facilitating access to financing and guarantees.
- **RegulatedMarket** — A market for trading securities referenced as the context for admission to trading.
- **Regulation** — A legal instrument governing filings and approvals under EU securities regulation referenced in the text.
- **RelatedPartyTransaction** — Transactions between the issuer and related parties, referenced in annex headings.
- **Remuneration** — Remuneration paid to people referred to in the French Monetary and Financial Code, approved by shareholders.
- **RemunerationCommittee** — A committee referenced in corporate governance annexes for remuneration matters.
- **RenewableEnergy** — Renewable energies referenced as the basis for long-term electricity supply guarantees.
- **RiskFactorsSection** — A section of the universal registration document covering risk factors.
- **SecuritiesAdmissionToTrading** — Admission of securities to trading on a regulated market, supported by the universal registration document and related securities notes.
- **SecuritiesNote** — A document that, together with the universal registration document, enables an offer to the public or admission to trading.
- **SecuritiesOffer** — An offer to the public of securities, for which the universal registration document may be used when completed by additional notes.
- **StatutoryAuditor** — An auditor referenced in relation to reports on company and consolidated accounts and special auditors’ reports on agreements.
  - **SpecialAuditorsReport** — A special statutory auditors’ report on agreements referred to by the French Commercial Code.
- **Subsidiary** — An entity considered as part of the group, referenced as important subsidiaries in the organisational structure.
- **SummaryDocument** — A summary document that may be required alongside the universal registration document for certain securities transactions.
- **TreasuryShares** — Shares held by the company itself, referenced in assumptions affecting dividend amounts.
- **UniversalRegistrationDocument** — A universal registration document filed with a competent authority (AMF) and used as a base for securities offers or admissions to trading.
  - **AmendmentToUniversalRegistrationDocument** — An amendment to a universal registration document, filed with the competent authority and updating or replacing specific sections.
