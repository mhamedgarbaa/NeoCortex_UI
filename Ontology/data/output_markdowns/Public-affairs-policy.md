Policy relating to the processing of personal data implemented in the

1. WHO IS THIS POLICY FOR?

This policy (the "Policy") is intended for all persons whose data is processed by Bpifrance, its entities or
persons acting directly or indirectly on its behalf, in the context of Bpifrance's activities in the field of public
affairs (e.g. the development of biographies of public and/or political figures - elected officials, ministers,
members of ministerial cabinets, etc.). representatives of public authorities in the broad sense, carrying out
stakeholder mapping, developing and implementing engagement plans, etc.).

To this end, Bpifrance is required to process personal data in this context, and is concerned by the provisions
applicable to the processing of personal data.

You are covered by this Policy if you ("You") are:

• A public and/or political figure - elected official, minister, member of ministerial cabinets,

representative of public authorities, (hereinafter the "Public Figure");

• A media personality (essayist, author); journalist, editor-in-chief, reporter, photographer, etc.) ;

(hereinafter the " Media personality");

• Any other person acting directly or indirectly on behalf of the public figure (e.g. advisor, chief of staff,
etc.) or media personality (assistant, image advisor, communication, etc.) involved in the connection
between Bpifrance and this person (hereinafter the "Employee");

When you provide personal data relating to other persons, it is your responsibility to duly inform these persons
of the transmission of their data to Bpifrance and to make available to them the information contained in this
Policy.

5. TO WHOM IS YOUR PERSONAL DATA DISCLOSED?

Your data is intended for Bpifrance and will also be communicated to the other entities of the Bpifrance group,
as soon as this transmission is necessary to enable these entities to meet their legal or regulatory obligations
or to ensure the deployment of the proposed system. This transmission of intra-group information does not in
any way exempt the entities of the Bpifrance group from the legal, regulatory or contractual confidentiality
obligations that apply to them with regard to third parties.

Bpifrance is committed to limiting the transfer of personal data outside the European Union. When such
transfers are to be implemented, Bpifrance relies on the basis of:

• a decision issued by the European Commission, attesting to the level of adequacy of the regulations

of the country to which your data is transferred;

• one of the derogations provided for by the GDPR (in particular when the transfers are implemented
because they are necessary for the conclusion or performance of a contract concluded in your interest
between Bpifrance and the legal person to which you are linked);

In other cases, Bpifrance ensures that the transfer is supervised in accordance with the GDPR and implements
appropriate guarantees with regard to the protection of personal data, by means of legal, technical and
organisational measures.

6. WHAT ARE YOUR RIGHTS?

In accordance with the Applicable Regulations, and subject to the conditions set out therein for the exercise of
these rights, you benefit from:

• The right to access, rectify, delete, and portability of your personal data.
• The right to request the limitation of the processing that concerns you and to object to receiving

commercial prospecting where applicable.

• The ability to provide Bpifrance with instructions relating to the fate of his or her personal data after

your death.

To exercise your rights, you can contact the data protection officer of the Bpifrance group, using the contact
details provided in Section 7 ("How to contact Bpifrance? ") below.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Commission Nationale de l'Informatique et des Libertés
(CNIL).
