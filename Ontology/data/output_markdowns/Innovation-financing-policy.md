Policy relating to the processing of personal data implemented in the
context of Bpifrance's Financing activity

1. WHO IS THIS POLICY FOR?

This policy (the "Policy") is intended for all persons whose data is processed by Bpifrance in the
context of its financing activities:

•
Innovation funding;
• Financing of the treasury;
• Financing of assets and intangibles (short-term financing);
• Financing of energy/environment projects.

You are covered by this Policy if you are ("You"):

• Legal representative, authorised person, project manager, point of contact, beneficial owner
or shareholder/partner in a company that Bpifrance plans to finance/support or any other
person whose data collection is necessary for the investigation of a transaction (hereinafter
the "Prospect");

• Legal representative, authorised person, project manager, point of contact, beneficial owner
or shareholder/partner in a company financed and/or supported by Bpifrance (i.e. with which
Bpifrance has concluded an aid contract or a loan contract) or any other person whose data
collection is necessary for the investigation of an operation and its follow-up (hereinafter the
"Financed Company");

• Any other person whose data collection is strictly necessary for Bpifrance in the context of the
examination of an application or the follow-up of a file, and in particular any guarantors and/or
operational contacts.

When you provide personal data relating to other persons, it is your responsibility to duly inform these
persons of the transmission of their data to Bpifrance and to make available to them the information
contained in this Policy.

Some schemes are the subject of specific information notices:

- With regard to the processing of personal data implemented by Bpifrance in the context
of its export financing activities, we invite you to consult the dedicated policy: Data
protection | Bpifrance - https://www.bpifrance.fr/protection-des-donnees

- Regarding financing guaranteed by Bpifrance, we invite you to consult the dedicated
policy: Data protection | Bpifrance https://www.bpifrance.fr/protection-des-donnees or
you refer to the documentation submitted as part of your financing application.

4. HOW IS YOUR PERSONAL DATA COLLECTED?

Your personal data is collected by Bpifrance directly from you or from a third party who has duly informed
you of the transmission of your information to Bpifrance.

In some cases, Bpifrance also collects personal data about you indirectly from:

• Other Bpifrance entities in compliance with legal, regulatory or contractual confidentiality

obligations that apply to third parties;

• Any other funder/funder, in particular the State, within the framework of the financing

mechanisms implemented by Bpifrance;

• Bpifrance partners in charge of all or part of the scheme to which you are applying ;(please refer

to the documentation for your scheme);

• Databases made available by authorities or official third parties (e.g. the Official Journal of the
French Republic, the Trade and Companies Register, databases managed by banking
supervisory authorities);
IT service providers whose solution integrates a database containing information about you.

•

In these cases, Bpifrance ensures that the indirect collection of your data is carried out in accordance
with the requirements of the GDPR.

5. TO WHOM IS YOUR PERSONAL DATA DISCLOSED?

Your data is intended for Bpifrance and will also be communicated:

• To the other entities of the Bpifrance group, as soon as this transmission is necessary to
enable these entities to meet their legal or regulatory obligations or to ensure the
deployment of the proposed system. This transmission of intra-group information does not
in any way exempt the entities of the Bpifrance group from the legal, regulatory or
contractual confidentiality obligations that apply to them with regard to third parties;

• Partner banks involved in the financing operation when Bpifrance acts as lead bank;

• To the professional advisors (advisors, experts, law firms) and auditors of Bpifrance,

• Where applicable, to the State and the services attached, to other donors, to local

authorities, and/or to the European Commission;

• The partners involved in the financing mechanism concerned;

•

IT service providers, or any other service provider (including lawyers and experts who give
an opinion on a project that has been the subject of a request for financing) or
subcontractors providing services in connection with all or part of the processing identified
above on behalf of Bpifrance;

• Service providers in charge of evaluating the financing mechanism concerned (including

external expertise and audits);

• To any French or European administrative, judicial or supervisory authority that needs to be
aware of it in the context of an administrative or judicial procedure that benefits from a right
of communication based on a legislative or regulatory provision.

Bpifrance is committed to limiting the transfer of personal data outside the European Union. When such

6. WHAT ARE YOUR RIGHTS?

In accordance with the Applicable Regulations, and subject to the conditions set out therein for the
exercise of these rights, you benefit from:
• The right to access, rectify, delete, and portability of your personal data.
• The right to request the limitation of the processing concerning you and to object to receiving

commercial prospecting.

• The ability to provide Bpifrance with instructions relating to the fate of his or her personal data after

your death.

To exercise your rights, you can contact the data protection officer of the Bpifrance group, using the
contact details provided in Section 7 ("How to contact Bpifrance? ") below.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Commission Nationale de l'Informatique et des
Libertés (CNIL).
