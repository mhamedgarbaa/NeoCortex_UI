Section 3.1.9.8 of the 2022 Universal Registration Document proposing to amend Bpifrance’s articles of
association is updated to include the proposed Articles of Association which will be submitted on 16 May
to the ordinary and extraordinary General Meeting of Bpifrance shareholders (as presented in section
3.1.9.8 of the 2022 Universal Registration Document and in (a) below) additional update regarding the
Bpifrance’s object (cf. (b) below).

a) Proposal to update the preamble of the Articles of Association

Law No. 2021-1774 of 24 December 2021 (known as the Rixain law) aiming to accelerate economic and
professional equality has conferred an additional mission on Bpifrance: to provide support to companies
committed to gender equality in the workplace. This task has been incorporated into Article 1A of
Ordinance No. 2005-722 of 29 June 2005 for the Banque Publique d'Investissement.

It is proposed to include this additional mission in the preamble of Bpifrance’s Articles of Association to
ensure they are perfectly consistent with the missions referred to in Article 1A of the aforementioned
ordinance.

As a result of the Rixain law, the title of Bpifrance's mission towards “female entrepreneurship” has been
updated to "entrepreneurship by women” (from entreprenariat féminin to “entreprenariat des femmes”). It
is proposed to reflect this change in the preamble of Bpifrance's Articles of Association.

"2.3

transferred to bodies providing securitisation, group funding, and credit institutions, particularly by

"2.3
As part of the activity defined in (2) of Article 2.1 above, the object of the Company, a public

businesses and individuals by sharing the risk of financial assistance granted by other intermediaries.
