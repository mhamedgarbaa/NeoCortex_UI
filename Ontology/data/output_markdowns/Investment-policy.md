1. WHO IS THIS POLICY FOR?

This policy (the "Policy") is intended for all persons whose data is processed by Bpifrance
Investissement, the management company of the Bpifrance group, or by investment vehicles managed
and/or advised by Bpifrance Investissement (existing or future companies or holding companies).

Indeed, Bpifrance Investissement is required to process personal data in the context of its various direct
or indirect investment activities, in equity and quasi-equity.

You are covered by this Policy if you are ("You"):

• Legal

representative, authorised person, point of contact, beneficial owner or
shareholder/partner in a company in which Bpifrance Investissement intends to invest or any
other person whose data collection is necessary for the examination of a transaction
(hereinafter the " Target Company ");

• Legal

representative, authorised person, point of contact, beneficial owner or
shareholder/partner in a company or in which Bpifrance Investissement has invested, or any
other person whose collection of data is necessary for the investigation of a transaction
(hereinafter the " Participation ");

• Legal representative, authorised person, point of contact, beneficial owner, subscriber of an
investment fund subscribed to by Bpifrance Investissement (hereinafter the "Partner Fund")

• Legal

shareholder/partner of a legal entity that has subscribed to one of the structures managed

• Legal

shareholder/partner of any stakeholder in a transaction carried out by a Fund Managed by
Bpifrance Investissement or any other investment vehicle (e.g. co-investor, corporate bank,
external advisor);

• Any other person whose data collection is strictly necessary for Bpifrance Investissement in

order to carry out its activities.

The purpose of the Policy is to inform you about the processing of personal data implemented
by Bpifrance Investissement in the context of its various activities.

When you provide personal data relating to other persons, it is your responsibility to duly inform these
persons of the transmission of their data to Bpifrance Investissement and to make available to them the
information contained in this Policy.

For certain specific activities, Bpifrance Investissement may be required to provide you, if necessary,
with additional information adapted to the processing implemented.

4. HOW IS YOUR PERSONAL DATA COLLECTED?

Your personal data is collected by Bpifrance Investissement directly from you or from a third party who
has duly informed you of the transmission of your information to Bpifrance Investissement.

In some cases, Bpifrance Investissement also collects personal data about you indirectly from:

• Other entities of the Bpifrance group in compliance with the legal, regulatory or contractual

confidentiality obligations that apply to third parties;

• Co-investors of Bpifrance Investissement or any other financier in the context of the

transactions;

• Databases made available by authorities or official third parties (e.g. the Official Journal of the
French Republic, the Trade and Companies Register, databases managed by financial sector
supervisory authorities);
IT service providers whose solution integrates a database that may hold information about you.
In these cases, Bpifrance Investissement ensures that the indirect collection of this data complies with
the GDPR.

5. TO WHOM IS YOUR PERSONAL DATA DISCLOSED?

Your data is intended for Bpifrance Investissement and may also be communicated:

• To other entities of the Bpifrance group, as soon as such transmission is necessary to
enable these entities to meet their legal or regulatory obligations. This transmission of intra-
group information does not in any way exempt the entities of the Bpifrance group from the
legal, regulatory or contractual confidentiality obligations that apply to them with regard to
third parties;

• To the professional advisors (advisors, experts, law firms, investment banks) and auditors
of Bpifrance Investissement, it being specified that there is no obligation to inform these
persons in this way if they are subject to professional secrecy or are otherwise bound by
confidentiality obligations;
IT service providers, or any other service provider (including lawyers) or subcontractors
providing services in connection with all or part of the processing identified above on behalf
of Bpifrance Investissement;

•

• To any French or European administrative, judicial, or supervisory authority that needs to
be aware of it in the context of an administrative or judicial procedure that benefits from a
right of communication based on a legislative provision, at their request.

In addition, your data may also be communicated in the following cases:

• With regard to the data of subscribers and potential subscribers:

• To the Depositary of the vehicle in which you have subscribed, who carries out the missions
incumbent on him in application of the laws and regulations in force as well as those
contractually entrusted to him by Bpifrance Investissement;

• To the Accounting Delegate of the vehicle in which you have invested, to whom Bpifrance

Investissement has delegated the accounting management of its funds;

• To the Statutory Auditor of the said vehicle, who, in particular, certifies the sincerity and

regularity of the accounts;

• To credit institutions in the event of the implementation of financing in the vehicle to which

you have subscribed;

• To the competent tax authorities, in order for Bpifrance Investissement to comply with its
legal obligations, including in particular those arising from the DAC 2 Directive as
transposed into French law and the Foreign Account Tax Compliance Act (FATCA).

• With regard to data collected in the context of investments in Target Companies and

Participations and subscriptions to Partner Funds:

•

In the context of investment operations (initial or additional) or divestment, to any co-
investors or any other financier within the limits of the confidentiality commitments made by
Bpifrance Investissement.

Bpifrance Investissement is committed to limiting the transfer of personal data outside the European
Union. When such transfers are to be implemented, Bpifrance Investissement relies on the following to
carry out these transfers:
• A decision issued by the European Commission, attesting to the level of adequacy of the regulations

of the country to which your data is transferred;

• One of the derogations provided for by the GDPR (in particular when the transfers are implemented
because they are necessary for the conclusion or performance of a contract concluded in your
interest between Bpifrance Investissement and the legal entity to which you are linked).

In other cases, Bpifrance Investissement ensures that the transfer is supervised in accordance with the
GDPR and implements the appropriate guarantees with regard to the protection of personal data, by
means of legal, technical and organisational measures.

6. WHAT ARE YOUR RIGHTS?

In accordance with the applicable regulations, and subject to the conditions set out therein for the
exercise of these rights, you benefit from:
• The right to access, rectify, delete, and portability of your personal data.
• The right to request the limitation of the processing concerning you and to object to receiving

commercial prospecting.

• The ability to provide Bpifrance Investissement with instructions relating to the fate of his or her

personal data in the event of death.

To exercise your rights, you can contact the data protection officer of the Bpifrance group, using the
contact details provided in Section 7 ("How to contact Bpifrance Investissement? ") below.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Commission Nationale de l'Informatique et des
Libertés (CNIL).
